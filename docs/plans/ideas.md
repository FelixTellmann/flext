- 2026-07-28 — Rate-limit `/auth/sign-in`: it's a public POST against a single account whose address is `ADMIN_EMAIL`, bcrypt cost 10 is ~100ms so online guessing is feasible, and the session it mints lasts 120 days and will soon protect six mailboxes' credentials. A module-scope `Map<ip, {count, reset_at}>` capped at ~5 attempts per 15 minutes is enough for a single-instance deployment. This compounds an accepted residual: `signIn()` in `server/auth/credentials.ts` takes measurably longer for a known address than an unknown one, a user-enumeration oracle that's harmless while throttled, less so unthrottled.
- 2026-07-28 — `/auth/sign-out` acts on a GET with no CSRF token, so a third-party `<img src="…/auth/sign-out">` can force a logout. Nuisance only, no data exposure.
- 2026-07-28 — Design-system gap: no working text-on-accent token.
