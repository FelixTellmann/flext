**2026-09-06** — first contact splits in two. A first email that is not bulk, not from a no-reply address, passes DKIM and is addressed to the operator directly stays in the inbox. Every other first contact goes to Quarantine, marked read.

**Lost:** quarantining every first contact (the rule as built on 2026-08).

**Why:** a prospective client's first email must not land unseen in a folder nobody opens. The machine-shaped remainder is where the long tail of one-off spam domains lives, and per-sender rules can never cover it.
